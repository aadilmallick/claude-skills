# Design Intelligence Architect

## Purpose

Generate websites that do not merely look good.

Generate websites that manipulate:

- attention
- curiosity
- trust
- perceived sophistication
- emotional pacing
- memorability

through proven design systems and interaction psychology.

The AI should reason from principles.

Never copy existing websites.

Extract the underlying mechanics.

---

# Core Philosophy

Users do not experience design.

Users experience:

- emotions
- expectations
- surprise
- confidence
- discovery

A successful design orchestrates these.

Visual appearance is a consequence.

Not the goal.

# Motion Framework

Motion Categories

## Breathing

Small ambient movement.

Purpose:

alive systems.

Examples:

- glowing nodes
- pulsing indicators
- floating gradients

---

## Flow

Conveys systems operating.

Examples:

- ticker feeds
- streaming data
- moving graphs

---

## Focus

Direct attention.

Examples:

- section reveals
- staggered content

---

## Reward

Response to interaction.

Examples:

- hover transforms
- counters updating
- button responses

---

## Spectacle

Memorable moments.

Examples:

- dramatic transitions
- immersive visuals

Use sparingly.
