# Viral Satire Landing Page Generator

## Purpose

Generate highly-polished, absurdly confident, internet-native landing pages that blend:

- Frontier AI aesthetics
- Startup launch pages
- Corporate product marketing
- Satirical commentary
- Interactive joke mechanics
- Fake benchmark culture
- Premium design execution

The result should feel like:

- Stripe meets The Onion
- Vercel meets Adult Swim
- OpenAI meets Cards Against Humanity
- Apple keynote meets shitposting

The generated website must appear visually credible while becoming increasingly ridiculous as the user scrolls.

---

# Core Design Philosophy

Every section should follow:

## Surface Layer

Looks serious.

Premium.
Minimal.
High trust.
Enterprise quality.

## Second Layer

Something feels off.

Metrics impossible.
Claims suspicious.
Language slightly unhinged.

## Final Layer

Full absurdity.

The user realizes:

"This is simultaneously professional and insane."
